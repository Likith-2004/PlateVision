# OCR. Both backends ship by default; OCR_ENGINE selects one at runtime.
# paddleocr is the default (see app/ocr.py for the benchmark behind that).
# Dropping either engine is safe if you pin OCR_ENGINE to the other:
#   paddleocr+paddlepaddle ~= 380MB installed, +231MB of downloaded models
#   easyocr                ~=  15MB installed, + 94MB of downloaded models
